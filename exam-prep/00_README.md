## How the professor thinks (do NOT skip this)

Prof. Mircea Ioan-Gabriel is **not** a typical CS professor. He sees AI as the latest step in a 2500-year chain:

> **Philosophy (idea) → Logic → Math → Algebra → Informatics → AI → ?**

He will ask you to **define** things precisely (algorithm, problem, information, entropy, intelligence) and to **trace concepts back to their origins** (Greek roots, who invented what, why).

If you only memorize formulas you will fail to impress him. If you can say "Algorithm comes from Al-Khwarizmi, ~780–850, who wrote the first book on algebra (al-jabr = reunion of broken parts)" — that's the level he respects.
