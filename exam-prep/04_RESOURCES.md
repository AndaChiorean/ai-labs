## 2. BOOKS HE MENTIONED

### ⭐⭐⭐ "Zero: The Biography of a Dangerous Idea" — Charles Seife (2000)
- He literally wrote "Read 'Zero' — Charles Seife" on the board.
- History of zero: Babylonians → India → Arabs → Europe.
- Connects math, philosophy, religion.
- **Why he loves it:** matches his "trace ideas back to origins" style.
- **Where:** Amazon (~$15), libraries, Audible.

### ⭐⭐ "Gödel, Escher, Bach: An Eternal Golden Braid" — Douglas Hofstadter (1979)
- Connects logic, art, music, recursion, consciousness, AI.
- Won the Pulitzer Prize.
- Long (~800 pages), but the first 200 pages cover Aristotelian → Boolean → Gödel chain perfectly.

### ⭐⭐ "The Selfish Gene" — Richard Dawkins (1976)
- Foundation for understanding genetic algorithms.
- The "gene's-eye view" of evolution.
- Especially Ch 11 ("Memes") for the idea of information evolution.

## 3. PAPERS HE NAMED OR ALLUDED TO

### ⭐⭐⭐ "Attention Is All You Need" — Vaswani et al., 2017
- He wrote this exact title on the board.
- Founded the Transformer architecture → GPT, BERT, ChatGPT, Claude.
- 8 authors at Google. Vaswani was first.
- **Free:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762
- **You don't need to read it fully** — just know the title, authors, year, and core idea (self-attention replaces RNNs).

### ⭐⭐ "A Mathematical Theory of Communication" — Claude Shannon, 1948
- The founding paper of information theory.
- Defines bit, entropy, channel capacity.
- **Free:** https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/home/text/others/shannon/entropy/entropy.pdf
- **Read:** sections 1–6.

### ⭐⭐ "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" — Alan Turing, 1950
- Proposes the Turing Test (Imitation Game).
- Hugely readable — Turing was a great writer.
- **Free:** https://academic.oup.com/mind/article/LIX/236/433/986238

### ⭐ "The Perceptron: A Probabilistic Model" — Frank Rosenblatt, 1958
- Original perceptron paper.
- More for context — algorithm details are in your study guide.

### ⭐⭐ "Induction of Decision Trees" — J.R. Quinlan, 1986
- The ID3 algorithm paper (which your decision_tree.py implements).
- **Where:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00116251

### ⭐ "Fuzzy Decision Trees" — Janikow, 1998 (and others)
- Foundation for the prof's PhD area.
- Search: "Janikow fuzzy decision trees" or "Olaru Wehenkel"
